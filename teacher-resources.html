Official Food Technology outcomes

OutcomeExact official wording
FT4-ADJ-01in Stage 4, teachers may adjust the Stage 5 outcomes as appropriate to the needs of students in Years 7 and 8
FT5-SAF-01applies safe work and management practices through food design and production processes
FT5-COM-01communicates ideas, concepts and solutions in food design and production contexts
FT5-FPP-01selects and justifies the use of food preparation and presentation practices
FT5-USE-01selects and applies ingredients, techniques and equipment in food design and production contexts
FT5-IET-01explains industry practices and emerging technologies used in food design and production
FT5-NSA-01assesses how nutrition, food selection and availability influence individuals and communities
FT5-ENV-01analyses relationships between food production systems, sustainability and society

What students are learning

Learning focusIn plain English
Safe food workApply hygienic routines, manage risks and follow teacher-approved kitchen controls.
CommunicationExplain food ideas, processes, evidence and decisions for a clear purpose and audience.
Preparation and presentationSelect and justify practices that improve safety, quality and presentation.
Ingredients, techniques and equipmentChoose and use suitable ingredients, techniques and equipment under teacher direction.
Industry and technologyExplain how food production roles and technologies influence contemporary practice.
Nutrition and selectionAssess how nutrition, availability and individual needs affect food choices.
Food systems and sustainabilityAnalyse how production, distribution, waste and society are connected.
Design and evaluationDevelop a purposeful food solution, test it with evidence and recommend improvements.
