  • School approval for 2026 early implementation and the course’s 100-hour or 200-hour relationship.
  • The practical calendar and which recipes are mandatory, optional or assessment choices.

  • The school’s chopping-board colour convention and any room-specific cleaning directions.
  • Assessment issue and due dates, weighting, outcomes, practical product and marking rubric.
  • Lawful local access to That Sugar Film; the film itself is not copied or embedded here.
