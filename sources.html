Sources and boundaries

The teacher-posted Google Drive and Classroom masters control the local course sequence, practical evidence and Party Task intent. Current official sources control syllabus, food-safety, nutrition and sustainability facts.

What controls this course?

When two sources disagree, the higher relevant authority controls. The conflict stays visible for staff rather than becoming a guessed student requirement.

  1. Current NESA syllabus and implementation advice. Steve confirmed the Food Technology 7–10 Syllabus (2025) for the school’s 2027 early implementation. This remains a Stage 4 introductory elective candidate until its timetable and formal course relationship are confirmed.
  2. Teacher-posted Drive and Classroom masters. The 20-week program, introductory recipe booklet, safety and nutrition lessons, Party Task notice/workbook, Party recipes, sensory source and classroom worksheets control local intent in their domains.
  3. Current official public guidance. NSW Food Authority, FSANZ, Eat for Health and Australian Government sources control current safety, nutrition and sustainability facts.
  4. Original course materials. New theory, diagrams, photographs, checks, activities, videos and presentations fill source-pack learning gaps without replacing teacher-controlled facts.
